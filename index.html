Mobile Guide

Mobile Version CMir4 Create Account Tutorial

Follow these steps to sign in to CMir4 Mobile or create a new account.

01
◆

Sign in with Google

Open the CMir4 mobile application. On the sign-in screen, find Sign in with Google.

What to do

Tap Sign in with Google to proceed with the account setup process. Follow any Google sign-in prompts displayed on your device.

◉ Step 1: Tap Sign in with Google.
02
◆

Choose Sign In or Create Account

After proceeding, you should see the Username and Password fields, a Sign In button, and the Create Account / Change Password option.

If you already have an account

Enter your existing username and password, then tap Sign In.

If you want to create an account

Tap Create Account / Change Password to continue to the account creation screen.

◉ Step 2: Sign in with your existing account or choose Create Account / Change Password.
03
◆

Enter Your Account Details

The Create Account screen contains Username, Password, and Invitation Key (if required).

How to fill in the form

1. Enter your desired username.
2. Enter your password.
3. Leave the Invitation Key blank.
4. Complete the account creation process using the option displayed in the app.

◉ Step 3: Enter your username and password. Leave the Invitation Key blank.
✦

You're Ready to Get Started!

Review your account details and follow the instructions shown in CMir4 Mobile to finish creating your account.
